Why We Built a Zero-Server PDF Studio

For over a decade, online PDF utilities operated on an outdated cloud-upload model: you upload a private tax return, legal contract, or medical record to an unknown third-party server, wait in a processing queue, and then get hit with a paywall when you try to download your file.

Modern web browsers are now powerful enough to run full vector rasterization and coordinate-aware text parsing locally. By combining Mozilla’s PDF.js Web Worker engine with 2.0x HiDPI HTML5 Canvas rendering, PDFDaVinci turns your own browser into a private, hardware-accelerated document workstation.

Our Core Capabilities

  • PDF to High-Resolution JPG (ZIP): 2x supersampled JPEG rendering with one-click batch ZIP archiving or individual page downloads.
  • PDF to Editable Word (.DOC): Coordinate-based paragraph reconstruction compatible with Microsoft Word, Google Docs, and LibreOffice.
  • PDF to Clean Plain Text (.TXT): Instant UTF-8 text extraction with a live in-browser editor, word/character counters, and one-click clipboard copy.
  • Interactive Page Gallery: Side-by-side visual page inspection and full-screen zoom preview.
